06.3 Transaction invariants I01–I12
        I01 — Unique live attempt per (episode_id, scene_id, output_slot) for a plan revision; no duplicate provider
         submit.
        I02 — Available(account) = max(fresh_observed_balance - SUM(active local reservations) - safety_hold, 0).
         Nilai ini estimasi kapasitas untuk dispatch, bukan saldo Google final.
        I03 — All reservations across ALL projects for a profile are written in ONE global authoritative DB
         transaction.
        I04 — Owner token/generation lease fencing: old workers cannot mark/charge/release jobs after losing
         ownership.
        I05 — If boundary SUBMIT_STARTED and remote outcome unknown, reservation stays
         HELD_PENDING_RECONCILIATION.
        I06 — CONFIRMED spent requires provider evidence; SAFE_FAILURE without evidence of no charge stays
         UNKNOWN.
        I07 — No provider action on policy=UNKNOWN, model unsupported, auth-not-ready, price stale, invalid
         input or insufficient usable credit.
